Getting a good analysis

What makes a good screenshot?

A real screenshot (not a photo of your screen) of a supported market, with candles, the price axis on the right and the timeframe visible (for example 3m or 15m).

Zoom to about 100 candles on each chart. Wickwise reads the trend and sets the stop, trigger and targets from the swing highs and lows it can see:

  • Under about 50 candles: too zoomed in. Too few swings to see the trend or place a sensible stop.
  • About 60 to 200 candles: best. The result shows "✓ Both charts are zoomed well".
  • Over about 250 candles: too zoomed out. Prices can't be read precisely.

If a chart is outside the range, the result says so and the setup is rated Weak at best.

Which second chart should I add?

A higher timeframe of the same market: 1m with 5m, 3m with 15m, 5m with 15m, 15m with 1H, 1H with 4H. Wickwise checks the two agree before it calls a direction. Both charts count as one analysis.

Why does it say “developing”, “No clear trend” or “No valid setup”?

Developing means the setup is there but no candle has closed beyond the trigger yet: Wickwise tells you what to wait for instead of forcing a trade. No clear trend means the entry chart has neither higher highs and higher lows nor lower highs and lower lows, so there's no plan, just the levels that would decide it. No valid setup means the rules couldn't find a sensible stop or target. If the higher timeframe doesn't confirm, the trade is still shown, with a lower quality rating and a label such as "No 4H confluence" or "Against 4H uptrend".

What do Good, Fair and Weak mean?

A rule-based rating of the setup, not a chance of winning. Good: clear trend, the higher timeframe agrees, the chart is well zoomed, and a target pays at least twice the risk (2R). Fair: one thing missing, for example no higher-timeframe confluence or the best target under 2R. Weak: something counts against it, such as trading against the higher-timeframe trend. The result always lists the reasons.

Where is the stop?

It's the Stop row in the trade plan, also named in the line under the verdict (e.g. "Stop above 1.33370"). It sits just beyond the last swing high for a short, or the last swing low for a long: the price where the idea is proven wrong.

Why is my plan shown in amber?

The reward:risk is poor: the first target is less than the risk to the stop. The plan is still shown so you can judge it, with a warning.

Which markets does it read?

Indices: US30 (Dow Jones), NAS100 (Nasdaq 100), US500 (S&P 500) and GER40 (DAX). Forex majors: EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, USDCHF, AUDUSD, USDCAD and NZDUSD. It recognises your broker's names for them, such as DE40, USTEC or EUR/USD. Other markets aren't analysed yet and don't use a free analysis. Tell us which you'd like next during setup or by email.

Subscriptions

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Wickwise. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends to avoid the next charge.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I paid but Pro isn't active

Open the Pro screen in the app and tap “Restore purchases”, using the same Apple ID you paid with. If it still doesn't work, email us.

Your data

Are my charts stored?

No. They're analysed, then discarded. Your history stays on your phone. See the Privacy Policy.

I lost my history

History is saved only on your phone, so it's removed if you delete the app. We can't recover it.
