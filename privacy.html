Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account.
  • Chart screenshots you choose are sent to our server to be analysed, then discarded. We don't store them.
  • Your analysis history and settings stay on your iPhone.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics tools, and we never sell data.
  • Apple handles payments. We never see your card details.

Who we are

Wickwise is an iPhone app that analyses US30 chart screenshots for educational purposes. In this policy “we” and “us” means Tom Bettle (trading as Wickwise), who is responsible for your data. You can contact us at support.wickwise@icloud.com.

What we collect and why

WhatWhyHow long
Chart screenshots you pick or photograph To analyse them and send back the result. This is the service you ask for. Not stored by us. Discarded once the analysis is returned.
Your settings sent with each analysis: trading style and explanation level So the analysis matches how you trade. Not stored by us.
Markets you'd like, picked during setup (for example Gold or NAS100) To decide which market to add next. Kept as an anonymous list with no link to you or your device.
Basic connection data: IP address, time of request and errors Recorded automatically by our hosting provider to keep the service running and secure. Short-term hosting logs, deleted automatically.

We don't collect your name, email address, location, contacts or device identifiers, and we don't build a profile of you.

What stays on your iPhone

Your analysis history (including small thumbnails of your charts), your settings, and how many free analyses you've used are saved only on your device. We can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Camera and photos

Wickwise asks for camera or photo library access only when you tap “Take photo” or “Choose screenshot”. Only the single image you pick is used. We never browse or upload anything else.

Who processes your data for us

  • Anthropic provides the AI model (Claude) that reads your chart. Your screenshot is sent to Anthropic's API to produce the analysis. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API data is not used to train their models. Anthropic may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring, as described in Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Vercel hosts our server, which receives your screenshot, passes it to Anthropic and returns the result. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • Apple handles subscriptions and payments through your Apple ID. We receive confirmation that you have a subscription, never your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

Some of these providers are based in the United States, so your data may be processed there. They use safeguards approved under UK and EU data protection law for these transfers.

Legal basis

Under UK and EU GDPR, we process your screenshots and settings because it's needed to provide the service you ask for (contract), and connection logs and anonymous market choices because we have a legitimate interest in running, securing and improving Wickwise.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad or analytics tools in the app.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of your data for marketing.
  • We don't use your charts to train AI models.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to how we use it. Because we don't store your screenshots or know who you are, there is usually nothing we hold that can be linked to you. To delete everything on your phone, delete the app. For any request, email support.wickwise@icloud.com.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or your local data protection authority.

Children

Wickwise is for adults (18+) and isn't intended for children. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app.
